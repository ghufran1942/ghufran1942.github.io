## Project Overview

As the Entrepreneurial Lead, I conducted 107 customer interviews over 6 weeks to evaluate market needs for a Deformable UAV prototype.

## Key Features

- Market exploration across 3 sectors
- Interviewed industry stakeholders
- Validated potential value propositions
- Informed pivot in product development

## Technical Implementation

Utilized lean startup methodology, mapped customer segments and decision-makers, and documented interview insights.

## Results

Determined strongest product-market fit in precision agriculture and underground mining. Team shifted focus to these verticals.
